How a BurnMate weekly plan is made

The server prepares food and exercise separately, merges them into one prompt, lets the AI choose from safe lists, then computes and checks every number itself. Click any box — the panel explains it in plain words and shows what happens for Asha, a sample user.

Safety first

A profile check runs before anything else.

No AI in the maths

Calories, macros and safe lists are pure server rules.

One AI call per part

Mon–Wed, then Thu–Sun. Gemini first, Groq if it fails.

Checked, then saved

Every day is graded; a miss gets one retry.
