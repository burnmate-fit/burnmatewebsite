How BurnMate works

Two flowcharts: Weekly plan — how the server turns your answers into meals and workouts (the AI only chooses; the server computes and checks every number) — and CM5 workout — how the TV box tracks you with MediaPipe, counts reps, coaches form and saves results. Click any box for a plain explanation, a worked example and the exact rules.
